{# trans Section: What each tool actually is #}

{% trans %}What each tool actually is{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}Otter records and transcribes your meetings through its bot, then files everything into a searchable workspace. It's been doing this longer than almost anyone, and search across months of conversations is the thing it does best. Think of it as a memory: for you first, and for your team if you share.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}Fireflies records the same way, then goes corporate. Transcripts flow into CRM records, calls get scored, managers get dashboards of talk time and topics across every rep. Think of it as infrastructure for a revenue team. The individual rep is not really the customer; the person running the team is.{% endtrans %}

{# trans Section: The free plans, honestly #}

{% trans %}The free plans, honestly{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}This is where most people start, so it's worth being precise. Otter's free plan is 300 minutes a month, which is about five one-hour meetings. Except it isn't, because each conversation stops at 30 minutes. If your calls run long, the free plan is really a trial.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}Fireflies' free plan transcribes without a monthly minute cap, which sounds better, but storage is 400 minutes for the whole team and AI summaries are limited. You'll hit the storage wall in a week or two of real use.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}My take: neither free plan is meant to be lived on. If free is the requirement, Fathom's free plan is more generous than both.{% endtrans %}
