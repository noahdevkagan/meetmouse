{# trans Section: What each tool actually does #}

{% trans %}What each tool actually does{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}Poised is an AI communication coach for your calls on Zoom, Meet, Teams and the rest. It gives real-time feedback on how you're speaking — filler words, pacing, hedging, confidence — plus post-meeting reports and trend lines so you can watch yourself improve over weeks. It's polished, it's established, and reviewers consistently say the feedback helps.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}MeetMouse coaches a different layer: not how you sound, but what's happening in the conversation. It applies a coaching rubric to the live discussion and cues you on substance — you've been monologuing for two minutes, that was a range not a commitment, sounds like agreement so close it out. Rubrics are yours to customize: sales calls, 1:1s, interviews, fundraising. And the whole thing — transcription and AI — runs on your Mac. No account, no cloud, works with WiFi off.{% endtrans %}

{# trans Section: Where MeetMouse wins #}

{% trans %}Where MeetMouse wins{% endtrans %}

  • {% trans %}It coaches the meeting, not the voice. You can deliver a flawless, filler-free monologue and still lose the deal because you never made the ask.
