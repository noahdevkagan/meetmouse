{# trans Section: The three questions that matter #}

{% trans %}The three questions that matter{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}1. Does a bot join the call? A visible recorder changes what people say — and in many contexts (two-party consent states, candidate calls) it's a legal and courtesy question too. Bot-free tools capture system audio on your machine instead.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}2. Does audio or transcript leave your machine? "No bot" does not mean "no cloud." Most bot-free tools still send meeting content to cloud AI for processing. Once it leaves, you're trusting someone else's retention policy, breach surface, and subpoena response.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}3. Who can see what's stored? Team workspaces mean your calls are readable by admins and managers. Sales tools pipe them into CRMs. Ask: who, besides me, can pull up this conversation?{% endtrans %}
