{# trans Section: What each tool actually does #}

{% trans %}What each tool actually does{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}Otter is the elder statesman of meeting AI. Its bot (OtterPilot) joins your Zoom, Teams, or Meet call, records it, transcribes it in real time, and files everything into a searchable cloud workspace. Months later you can search "pricing discussion with Acme" and find the exact sentence. That archive is the product.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}MeetMouse doesn't build a cloud archive and doesn't send a bot. It runs quietly on your Mac, transcribes the conversation, writes the summary and next steps afterward, and during the call surfaces short coaching cues in an overlay only you can see — talk less, land the ask, pin down that vague commitment. Everything runs on your machine. When the meeting ends, nothing has left your Mac.{% endtrans %}
