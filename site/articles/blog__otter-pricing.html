{% trans %}300 minutes a month. About ten 30-minute calls. Fine for light use, gone in a week for anyone who lives in meetings.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}Your 25 most recent conversations. Otter's whole strength is searching your history, and the free plan only keeps the last 25. You'd pay for Pro to get the thing Otter is best at.{% endtrans %}
