{# trans Section: What each tool actually does #}

{% trans %}What each tool actually does{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}Granola sits quietly on your Mac (no bot joins the call), captures the audio, and blends your rough typed notes with an AI-written summary after the meeting. The output is a clean document: decisions, action items, context. It's a memory tool.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}MeetMouse also sits quietly on your Mac with no bot — but it works in the opposite direction. While you're talking, it applies a coaching rubric to the live conversation and surfaces short cues in a small overlay: you're monologuing, you skipped the ask, that "sounds good" wasn't actually a commitment. The overlay never shows up in screen shares. It's a behavior tool.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}One tells you what happened. The other changes what happens.{% endtrans %}
