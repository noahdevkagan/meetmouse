{# trans Section: What each tool actually does #}

{% trans %}What each tool actually does{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}Fireflies sends its bot into your meetings, records and transcribes them, then does team-scale things with the result: syncs to Salesforce or HubSpot, tracks topics across hundreds of calls, scores reps against talk-time benchmarks, feeds manager dashboards. The buyer is usually a sales leader; the product is visibility into the team's conversations.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}MeetMouse has no bot, no dashboard for your boss, and no cloud. It listens to your live meeting on your Mac and gives you — only you — short cues in a screen-share-safe overlay while there's still time to act on them. Nobody else ever sees your calls, your stats, or your bad habits. The whole point is that improvement is easier when it's private.{% endtrans %}
