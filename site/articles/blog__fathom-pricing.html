Yes, Team is cheaper per seat than Premium. That's because Team needs at least two seats. If it's just you, Premium is the plan; if there are two of you, Team at $38 a month beats two Premium seats at $40.

What the free plan is good enough for

Honestly, most individuals. Unlimited recordings with a decent summary after every call is more than Otter's free plan (300 minutes a month, 30 minutes per conversation) or Granola's (30 days of history) give you. If you take a handful of calls a week and want a record, stop reading and sign up.

You'd pay for Premium if you want the better summaries and action items, or you want to ask questions across your calls. You'd pay for Team or Business when other people need to see your calls.

The costs that aren't on the pricing page

The bot. By default a Fathom bot joins every call. Most people don't care. Some guests do, and you won't always know which ones. Fathom lists bot-free capture as a beta, which is worth checking if this is your only complaint.

The cloud. Every recording lives on Fathom's servers. For a lot of calls that's fine. For client, candidate, legal or medical conversations, it's a question your company should answer before you hit record.

When I'd pick something else

If the bot or the cloud is the problem, MeetMouse is the one I built: it listens on your Mac, so nothing joins the call, and transcription and AI run locally. It also nudges you during the call (talk less, land the ask), which no summary does. Free for 3 meetings a week; $20 once for unlimited. It's per person with no team workspace, so it doesn't replace Fathom Business for a sales org.
