{# trans Section: What the free plan is good enough for #}

{% trans %}What the free plan is good enough for{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}Honestly, most individuals. Unlimited recordings with a decent summary after every call is more than Otter's free plan (300 minutes a month, 30 minutes per conversation) or Granola's (30 days of history) give you. If you take a handful of calls a week and want a record, stop reading and sign up.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}You'd pay for Premium if you want the better summaries and action items, or you want to ask questions across your calls. You'd pay for Team or Business when other people need to see your calls.{% endtrans %}

{# trans Section: The costs that aren't on the pricing page #}

{% trans %}The costs that aren't on the pricing page{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}The bot. By default a Fathom bot joins every call. Most people don't care. Some guests do, and you won't always know which ones. Fathom lists bot-free capture as a beta, which is worth checking if this is your only complaint.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}The cloud. Every recording lives on Fathom's servers. For a lot of calls that's fine. For client, candidate, legal or medical conversations, it's a question your company should answer before you hit record.{% endtrans %}
