{# trans Section: The 30-day catch #}

{% trans %}The 30-day catch{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}The free plan doesn't limit how many meetings you take notes on. It limits how far back you can see. That's a smart pricing choice: you get hooked on the notes in week one, and in week five you go looking for that call with the investor and it's behind the paywall.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}If you only care about this week's meetings, free is fine forever. If your notes are a record you come back to, plan on Business.{% endtrans %}

{# trans Section: Is Granola worth paying for? #}

{% trans %}Is Granola worth paying for?{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}For a lot of people, yes. The notes are genuinely good, nobody named Granola joins your call, and $14 is less than most bot notetakers. If the bot was your only problem with Fathom or Otter, this is probably your answer.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}The one thing to know: Granola captures audio on your computer, but the notes are written with cloud AI. So it fixes the bot, not the cloud. For calls where nothing should leave your laptop, that matters.{% endtrans %}
