Granola pricing in 2026: the free plan, the 30-day catch, and what Business costs

Noah KaganFounder, MeetMouse · Last checked October 20, 2026

Full disclosure: I make MeetMouse, and Granola is the competitor I respect most. No bot, great notes. Here's what it costs, read off granola.ai on October 20, 2026.

Quick answer

Granola Basic is free and takes AI notes on your meetings, but you can only see the last 30 days of meeting history. Business is $14 per user per month and adds unlimited notes and history, advanced AI models, and integrations like Notion, HubSpot, Attio and Zapier. Enterprise is $35 per user per month with admin controls. There's no bot on any plan; notes are written with cloud AI.

The 30-day catch

The free plan doesn't limit how many meetings you take notes on. It limits how far back you can see. That's a smart pricing choice: you get hooked on the notes in week one, and in week five you go looking for that call with the investor and it's behind the paywall.

If you only care about this week's meetings, free is fine forever. If your notes are a record you come back to, plan on Business.

Is Granola worth paying for?

For a lot of people, yes. The notes are genuinely good, nobody named Granola joins your call, and $14 is less than most bot notetakers. If the bot was your only problem with Fathom or Otter, this is probably your answer.

The one thing to know: Granola captures audio on your computer, but the notes are written with cloud AI. So it fixes the bot, not the cloud. For calls where nothing should leave your laptop, that matters.

Questions

Is Granola free?
Yes. The Basic plan is $0 and includes AI meeting notes, AI chat within and across meetings, shared folders and templates. The catch is history: you can only see meetings from the last 30 days.
How much is Granola Business?
$14 per user per month. It adds unlimited meeting notes and history, access to more advanced AI models, and integrations with Attio, Notion, HubSpot, Affinity and Zapier, plus API access.
What happens to my Granola notes after 30 days on the free plan?
Granola's comparison table lists meeting history as 30 days on Basic and unlimited on paid plans. Upgrading to Business gets you unlimited history. Check Granola's help docs for how older notes are handled before you rely on them.
