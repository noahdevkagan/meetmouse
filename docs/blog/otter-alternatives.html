Otter.ai alternatives: what to use instead (from a guy who competes with them)

Disclosure first: I make a meeting tool (MeetMouse, $20 one time, does a different job than Otter). Weird horse in this race, which is exactly why I can be straight with you about the other horses.

TL;DR

Leaving Otter over the thin free plan? Fathom is free with no minute cap. Leaving because a bot announces itself in every call? Granola takes notes with no bot. Leaving because your calls shouldn't live in anyone's cloud — or because the transcript was never really the point? That's MeetMouse: $20 once, runs 100% on your Mac, and coaches you live instead of filing the meeting away.

People usually search "Otter alternative" for one of three reasons: the free plan's 300 minutes a month evaporates in a week of real meetings and $16.99/month forever feels bad; the "Otter.ai has joined the meeting" announcement makes every call slightly more awkward; or it finally registers that every word of every meeting is sitting in someone else's cloud. Different problems, different answers. Here's the honest map.

1. Fathom — if you want Otter's job done free

The most direct replacement. Fathom records, transcribes, and summarizes with a genuinely free individual plan — no minute cap, which is exactly where Otter's free tier hurts. The trade you're making is the same one you had with Otter: a visible bot joins your calls and your meetings live in their cloud. If neither bothered you and price was the issue, stop reading and get Fathom.

2. Granola — if the bot is the problem

Granola captures system audio on your Mac — no participant named "Granola" ever appears, nobody talks differently. Notes are generated with cloud AI, so it's not a privacy play, but it kills the announced-bot awkwardness completely. Free until the 25-note history cap bites; $14/user/month after. I wrote a whole comparison with Granola — it's the tool I respect most on this list.

3. Fireflies — if you're actually a sales team

If your real complaint is that Otter doesn't push into your CRM or score calls across reps, you've outgrown it sideways — Fireflies is that machine, from $18/user/month. For an individual it's overkill wearing a suit. Full comparison here.

4. tl;dv — if you share moments, not transcripts

Free unlimited recordings and the best clip-sharing on the list — grab the thirty seconds where the customer said the thing, send it to your team. The catch: free recordings self-delete after 3 months. A free tool with a built-in upgrade timer. Respect, but know what you're signing.

5. The scrappy $0 option

QuickTime (or your phone's voice memos), then drop the file into ChatGPT or Claude: "summarize this with action items." Manual, slightly annoying, costs nothing, no bot, and the output is better than you'd guess. I did this for months.

6. MeetMouse — if the transcript was never the point

Here's the thing I noticed after years of transcripts: I never got better. The archive documented me talking too much with perfect fidelity. MeetMouse is what I built instead — it listens live on your Mac and nudges you during the call: talk less, land the ask, pin down that vague "sounds good." No bot, no account, no cloud; transcription and AI run entirely on-device and it works with WiFi off. $20 once. It deliberately doesn't build a searchable archive — if you need one, pair it with Fathom or Granola. Both run happily alongside.

So which one?

  • Otter's job, free: Fathom.
  • No bot in the room: Granola.
  • Sales team with a CRM: Fireflies.
  • Nothing leaves your Mac, and you want to get better — not just documented: MeetMouse, $20 once.

Questions

What is the best free alternative to Otter.ai?
Fathom. Its free individual plan has no minute cap — Otter's free tier stops at 300 minutes a month. The trade-off is the same as Otter's: a visible bot joins your calls and recordings live in Fathom's cloud.
Is there an Otter alternative without a bot joining the meeting?
Yes — Granola takes notes by capturing system audio on your Mac, so no bot ever appears in the call. MeetMouse also works bot-free, and goes further: nothing is uploaded at all — transcription and AI run entirely on your Mac.
Is there an Otter alternative that works offline?
MeetMouse is the only tool on this list that works with WiFi off — transcription and coaching run 100% on-device. Every other option, including Otter, processes audio in the cloud.
Does any Otter alternative avoid a subscription?
MeetMouse is $20 one time — no subscription, no account. Everything else on the list is free-tier-plus-monthly-subscription.
