You know the moment. The call connects, and then: "Fireflies.ai Notetaker has joined the meeting." Your candidate stiffens. Your prospect asks what it is. Your report gets a little more careful. Whatever the tool captures after that is a slightly worse conversation than the one you would have had.

How bot-free capture actually works

Meeting bots exist because they're easy to build: a headless participant joins the call like any guest and records what it hears. The bot-free approach skips the meeting entirely and captures system audio on your computer — the same sound coming out of your speakers — plus your mic. Nothing joins; the other side sees nothing. On a Mac this uses the screen-capture audio permission, which is why bot-free tools ask for it.

One honest caveat: bot-free means invisible to participants. Recording-consent norms and laws still apply to you regardless of the mechanism — disclosure is about ethics and law, not about whether a bot avatar appears.

Poised — bot-free delivery coaching

No bot, but audio is processed through Poised's cloud. Coaches your delivery — filler words, pace, energy — on a subscription. Full comparison.

MeetMouse — bot-free and cloud-free

Mine. Same system-audio approach, but transcription and AI run entirely on your Mac — turn WiFi off and it keeps working. No account, no telemetry, transcript saved to your Documents folder. The DIY option

QuickTime records your screen audio locally, no bot involved; paste the file into ChatGPT or Claude afterward for a summary. Free, manual, and your audio still goes to an AI provider at the paste step — but you choose which one and when.

Questions

Which AI notetakers don't use a bot?
Granola (notes via cloud AI), Poised (cloud coaching), and MeetMouse (fully on-device coaching) all capture system audio on your computer instead of joining the call. Otter, Fireflies, Fathom, and tl;dv all send a visible bot.
How do no-bot meeting tools capture the audio?
They record system audio — the sound your computer is already playing — plus your microphone, locally on your machine. Nothing joins the meeting, so participants see nothing.
Is it legal to use a notetaker that doesn't announce itself?
Bot or no bot, recording-consent laws still apply to you. Many teams disclose verbally regardless of tool. For regulated or two-party-consent situations, ask counsel — the bot avatar was never the legally relevant part.
