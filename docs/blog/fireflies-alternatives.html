Fireflies.ai alternatives: what to use instead (from a guy who competes with them)

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse ($20 one time, different job than Fireflies), so read me with that in mind. I'll point you at competitors anyway — most people searching this shouldn't buy my thing.

TL;DR

If Fireflies feels like overkill for one person, Fathom does the individual job free. If the bot-in-every-call is the issue, Granola takes notes without one. And if what actually bothers you is your conversations living in a company dashboard, MeetMouse is the opposite of that: a private, on-device coach only you can see, $20 once.

Fireflies is infrastructure for sales orgs: record every call, transcribe it, score it, push it to the CRM, let managers review talk-time across the team. People go looking for alternatives from two very different directions. Direction one: you're an individual and $18/user/month for enterprise machinery is absurd. Direction two: you're on a team using it and something about every conversation being recorded, scored, and reviewable started to itch. Both are fair. Different fixes.

1. Fathom — the individual's answer

If you're one person who needs recordings, transcripts, and summaries, Fathom's free plan does the job Fireflies charges $18/user/month for — genuinely free, no minute cap. Team features exist from $19/user/month when you need them. Still a visible bot, still cloud storage, but as a Fireflies replacement for individuals it's the obvious first stop.

2. Otter — if search is the feature you'd miss

Fireflies' archive with less CRM machinery. Otter's been at this longest and the search across months of transcripts is excellent. Free plan is thin (300 min/month); Pro is $16.99/month. My full Otter comparison.

3. Granola — notes with no bot in the room

Nothing joins the call. Granola captures system audio on your Mac and writes notes with cloud AI. If what soured you on Fireflies was the recorded-participant dynamic — guests going guarded the moment the notetaker appears — this fixes exactly that. Free until the 25-note history cap; $14/user/month after.

4. tl;dv — the budget team option

Free unlimited recordings, great moment-clipping for async teams, Pro at $29/month total (not per seat) undercuts Fireflies for small teams. Free recordings delete after 3 months — the shot clock is the business model.

5. The scrappy $0 option

Record with QuickTime or voice memos, paste into ChatGPT or Claude, ask for a summary with action items. No bot, no seat licenses, no dashboard. Clunky and free.

6. MeetMouse — the anti-Fireflies

Fireflies' model: record everything, analyze after, often reviewed by someone above you. MeetMouse inverts every part of that. It coaches during the meeting — talk balance, buried asks, vague commitments — in an overlay only you can see, and nothing is recorded to any cloud, ever. Transcription and AI run 100% on your Mac; it works with WiFi off. No bot, no account, $20 once. If Fireflies felt like being watched, this is what being helped feels like. Full head-to-head here.

So which one?

  • Individual who needs the basics free: Fathom.
  • Transcript search addict: Otter.
  • No bot, still want notes: Granola.
  • Small team, small budget: tl;dv.
  • Private improvement instead of org surveillance: MeetMouse, $20 once.

Questions

What is the best free alternative to Fireflies?
For individuals, Fathom — free with no minute cap, covering recording, transcription, and summaries. Fireflies' own free plan exists but limits AI credits.
Is there a Fireflies alternative that doesn't send a bot into meetings?
Granola takes notes with no bot by capturing system audio on your Mac. MeetMouse is also bot-free and keeps everything on-device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
Do any Fireflies alternatives coach you during the call?
MeetMouse does — live cues (talk balance, buried asks, vague commitments) in an overlay only you see, while the meeting is happening. Fireflies and the other notetakers analyze calls after they end.
What's the cheapest Fireflies alternative for a small team?
tl;dv — Pro is $29/month flat rather than per seat, versus Fireflies from $18 per user per month. For pure individual use, Fathom free beats both.
