1. MeetMouse — works in any client's meeting, keeps their info local

Because MeetMouse listens through your Mac's audio, it works in the client's Teams, Zoom, Webex, or Meet without joining — IT has nothing to block. Transcription and the default AI model run on-device, so client information stays on your laptop. After each call: topic-by-topic notes with owners, and an encrypted, expiring share link if you want to send the client a recap (just the curated notes — never the transcript).

The live part is the underrated bit for consultants: cues when a stakeholder raised a concern you haven't addressed, when you've been presenting too long, or when "let's circle back" wasn't a real commitment. $20 once — cheaper than one billable hour.

2. Granola — bot-free, cloud-based

Granola also avoids the bot, and its notes are polished. The trade is cloud processing, which some NDAs and client security reviews won't love. Check your contracts.

3. Fathom — for your own firm's calls

For internal meetings and your own sales calls, Fathom's free plan is great value. In client-run meetings, a bot is often unwelcome.

4. Otter — for long engagements

If you need a searchable archive across a months-long engagement and the client is fine with cloud tools, Otter's search is strong.

So which one?

  • Client meetings under NDA: MeetMouse.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use an AI notetaker on a client's Teams or Zoom meeting?

Bot-based tools need to join the client's meeting and are often blocked or unwelcome. MeetMouse and Granola capture audio on your Mac, so they work in any client-run meeting without joining.

Is using a cloud AI notetaker a breach of my NDA?

It can be, depending on the NDA's language about third parties and where the vendor processes data. MeetMouse avoids the question by keeping transcription and AI on your Mac by default. Check your specific agreements.

Can I share meeting notes with a client from MeetMouse?

Yes. One click creates an encrypted, expiring link containing only the curated notes — not the transcript or coaching — and you can revoke it anytime.

Fathom is free, Granola is $14/user/month after 25 notes, Otter Pro is $16.99/month, and MeetMouse is $20 one time.
