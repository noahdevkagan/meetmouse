The best AI notetaker for coaches

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: Coaching sessions are the worst place for a bot and the cloud. MeetMouse captures on your Mac, keeps sessions local, writes notes after — and nudges you when you're talking more than your client. $20 once. Granola if cloud notes are fine. Fathom if free is the only requirement.

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse. Every good coach I've worked with says some version of the same thing: the client should be doing most of the talking.

Which makes it funny that coaches' tools just take notes. Your session notes won't tell you that you jumped in with advice at minute six instead of asking one more question. And your clients are telling you real stuff — career fears, money, family. A bot in the corner changes what they say.

1. MeetMouse — notes, privacy, and a coach for the coach

Mine. It listens through your Mac, so no bot shows up in your client's Zoom. Sessions are transcribed and summarized on your laptop — nothing uploaded, no account. After each session you get notes with themes and commitments, and next week you can ask "what did she say she'd try?" and jump to the exact moment.

During the session, it keeps you honest: a cue when you've out-talked your client for a stretch, when they raised something and you moved on, or when "I'll try" wasn't really a commitment. The rubric is editable — put your own coaching principles in it. $20 once. If it doesn't change how your meetings go, email me within 30 days and I'll refund you.

2. Granola — great notes, cloud

If you just want clean session notes and don't mind the cloud, Granola is lovely and doesn't add a bot.

3. Fathom — free

Free and good, but a visible bot in a vulnerable conversation is a lot to ask of a client.

4. Poised — if you're working on your delivery

Poised coaches filler words and pace in real time. Useful if you're polishing how you sound; it doesn't look at the substance of the session. My comparison.

Frequently asked questions

What's the best AI notetaker for coaching sessions?

MeetMouse if you want sessions to stay on your Mac and live feedback on your own talk time. Granola for bot-free cloud notes. Fathom if you need free.

Will my client see a bot?

Not with MeetMouse or Granola — both capture audio on your computer. Fathom, Otter, and Fireflies join as a participant. Let clients know you take AI notes either way.

Can AI help me become a better coach?

That's the point of MeetMouse's live cues: talk balance, things your client raised that you skipped, and vague commitments — while the session is still happening.

Is MeetMouse for therapy or clinical notes?

No. It isn't built as a clinical tool. If you're a licensed clinician, use something designed for your regulatory requirements.
