What makes advisor meetings different

A sales call is about a deal. A review meeting is about someone's whole life: the account balances, the inheritance from a parent who just died, the divorce, the diagnosis that changes the retirement plan. People say these things out loud to you because they trust you. Every notetaker sends that conversation somewhere, and the honest way to pick one is to ask where.

There are only three places it can go: a vendor built for advisors, a general notetaker's cloud, or nowhere at all because it stays on your laptop. All three can be the right answer.

Four questions to ask before a client meeting

Does the audio or transcript leave my computer, and which companies see it? Does a bot show up on my client's screen? Do the notes need to land in my CRM or my firm's archive to count as a record? Can I delete a meeting for real if a client asks?

If your firm treats meeting notes as records you must keep, that answer alone points you to an advisor-built tool or to whatever compliance already approved. Don't guess on this one.

Questions

What is the best AI notetaker for financial advisors?
If your firm needs notes synced into a wealth CRM and kept as central records, an advisor-built tool like Jump or Zocks, approved by compliance. If you're independent and want client details kept off other companies' servers, MeetMouse, which runs transcription and AI on your Mac and is free for 3 meetings a week.
Can financial advisors use AI notetakers?
Many do, but recordkeeping and privacy rules vary by firm and regulator, so ask compliance first. Advisor-built tools focus on CRM and records; MeetMouse keeps client data on your Mac.
Which AI notetaker keeps client financial data private?
MeetMouse processes transcription and its default AI on your Mac and works with Wi-Fi off, so client details aren't uploaded. If you add your own Claude or OpenAI key, meeting text goes to that provider, so leave it on local for client meetings.
Does MeetMouse sync to Redtail, Wealthbox or Salesforce?
No. Notes stay on your Mac. You can copy them into your CRM or share them through an encrypted, expiring link.
Does a bot join client meetings?
Not with MeetMouse or Granola, which capture audio on your computer. Otter and Fireflies join as a visible participant, and Fathom does by default.
Is MeetMouse compliant with SEC or FINRA rules?
I can't tell you that, and you should be wary of any notetaker that says yes without your compliance team in the loop. MeetMouse has no compliance certifications. What it does is keep the conversation on your Mac by default, which is a different thing.
