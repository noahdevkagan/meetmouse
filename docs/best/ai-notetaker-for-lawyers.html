The best AI notetaker for lawyers

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: For client calls, pick the tool where the conversation never reaches anyone else. That's MeetMouse — transcription and AI on your Mac, no bot, works offline, $20 once. If your firm has vetted a cloud vendor, Granola or Otter on a business plan can work. A free bot on a privileged call is the one move I'd skip.

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse. I'm also not a lawyer, and this isn't legal or ethics advice — your bar and your firm get the final say. What I can tell you is how these tools actually move data, because that's the part the marketing pages get fuzzy about.

Lawyers don't need the prettiest summary. They need to answer one question: who else hears this conversation? A cloud notetaker sends it to the vendor, and usually to an AI company behind them. A bot notetaker also shows up in the meeting and may email a recap to everyone on the invite. Including the other side, if they're on it. Fun.

1. MeetMouse — nothing leaves the laptop

Mine. It hears the call through your Mac, so no bot joins. Transcription runs on-device and the default AI runs locally — pull the Wi-Fi mid-call and it keeps working. You get notes with next steps after, and you can ask a saved meeting "what did they say about the indemnity cap?" and get the timestamp. No MeetMouse account, no MeetMouse server holding your client's secrets.

Two things to know. You can add your own Claude or OpenAI key for a bigger model — then meeting text goes to that provider, so leave it on local for client work. And it's a personal Mac tool: no matter management, no firm-wide archive.

2. MacWhisper — for recordings

If you already record (with consent) and just need text, MacWhisper transcribes on your Mac. Nothing uploaded, nothing fancy.

3. Granola or Otter — through the firm, not a personal account

Both are good products and both process your conversations in their cloud. If IT and your ethics counsel have looked at the terms, retention, and sub-processors, and you're on a business plan, reasonable. Just don't run client matters through a personal free account.

4. Fathom — great product, wrong default here

I recommend Fathom all the time. Not for privileged calls: visible bot, cloud storage, recap emails.

Five questions to ask any vendor

  • Does audio or transcript text ever leave my device? To whom?
  • Which AI companies see it, and do they keep it?
  • What's the default retention, and can I actually delete it?
  • Does it auto-email recaps to attendees?
  • Does it announce itself in the meeting?

So which one?

  • Solo and small-firm lawyers who want AI notes with no third party: MeetMouse.
  • Transcribing recorded interviews: MacWhisper.
  • Firms with a vetted enterprise contract: Granola or Otter.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe for lawyers to use AI notetakers?

It depends on where the conversation goes. Cloud notetakers send it to the vendor and often to AI sub-processors. On-device tools like MeetMouse keep it on your Mac by default. Check your jurisdiction's ethics guidance and your firm's policies — this isn't legal advice.

Which AI notetaker doesn't send client calls to the cloud?

MeetMouse runs transcription and its default AI on your Mac and works with Wi-Fi off. MacWhisper transcribes recordings locally. Granola, Otter, Fathom, and Fireflies process in their cloud.

Do I still need consent?

Yes. Recording and transcription consent rules vary by jurisdiction. On-device processing changes who receives the data, not your consent obligations.

Can I use MeetMouse for depositions?

Use a court reporter for the official record. MeetMouse is for your own live calls and notes — prep sessions, client intakes, negotiations.
