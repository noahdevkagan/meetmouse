The best AI notetaker for recruiters and hiring managers

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: For high-volume teams that need ATS sync, a recruiting-focused cloud tool or Fireflies/Fathom with integrations. For bot-free candidate calls, Granola. For hiring managers who want to run better interviews — talk less, let candidates finish — MeetMouse, live, on-device, $20 once.

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse. I've also hired a lot of people, and the worst interviews I ran weren't under-documented — they were the ones where I talked for 30 of 45 minutes and then "didn't get a strong signal."

Recruiting has two tool problems. Recruiters need throughput: notes into the ATS without typing. Hiring managers need better interviews: more candidate talk, better follow-ups, fewer leading questions. And both need to think about candidates, who are often nervous and notice when a bot named "Notetaker" joins.

1. MeetMouse — better interviews, not just better notes

MeetMouse watches the live conversation and nudges you when you've been talking too long, when the candidate got cut off, or when you asked a yes/no question where a story would tell you more. The coaching rubric is editable, so you can add the interviewing habits you care about. No bot joins, so candidates don't see a recorder, and nothing about the candidate leaves your Mac by default.

After the interview: notes with topics and next steps, and a chat to ask "what did they say about managing up?" with a timestamp. No ATS integration — it's for the interviewer, not the pipeline.

2. Fireflies — throughput for recruiting teams

Transcribes screens and pushes notes to your systems. A bot joins, data lives in their cloud; check your candidate privacy notices.

3. Fathom — free, good summaries

Excellent free summaries for screens. Visible bot; some candidates find it offputting in early-stage conversations.

4. Granola — bot-free notes

If you want written notes without a bot joining candidate calls, Granola is the polished option. Cloud AI; $14/user/month past the free 25 notes.

A note on candidate data

Interview notes contain personal data about people who aren't your customers and didn't sign your vendor's terms. Fewer copies in fewer clouds is the conservative default. Tell candidates when you're using AI note-taking.

So which one?

  • Recruiting team, high volume, needs integrations: Fireflies or Fathom Team.
  • Bot-free written notes: Granola.
  • Hiring managers who want to run sharper interviews: MeetMouse.

Frequently asked questions

What's the best AI notetaker for interviews?

For recruiting teams needing integrations, Fireflies or Fathom. For bot-free interview notes, Granola. For hiring managers who want live coaching during the interview, MeetMouse.

Do candidates see the AI notetaker?

Bot-based tools (Fireflies, Fathom, Otter) join as a visible participant. MeetMouse and Granola capture audio on your Mac, so nothing joins the call. You should still tell candidates you use AI notes.

Can I customize the coaching for interviews?

In MeetMouse, yes — the coaching rubric is editable, so you can add the interviewing habits you want to be nudged on.

Is candidate data stored in the cloud?

With cloud notetakers, yes. MeetMouse keeps transcripts and notes on your Mac by default and has no account or server-side storage.
