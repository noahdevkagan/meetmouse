The best AI meeting assistant for sales calls

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: If you're a sales org that needs call scoring and pipeline analytics, Gong (big budget) or Fireflies (small budget). For free call notes into your CRM, Fathom. If you're the one on the call and you want to catch the buried objection before the call ends, MeetMouse — live cues, $20 once, nothing leaves your Mac.

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse. I also spent years selling — AppSumo deals, partnerships, sponsorships — and the calls I lost were almost never lost in the recap. They were lost at minute nine when the buyer said "I'd need to run this by finance" and I kept pitching features.

That's the split in this category. Most sales AI works after the call: transcribe, summarize, push to the CRM, let a manager score it next week. That's valuable for the org. A smaller set works during the call, when the rep can still change the outcome.

1. MeetMouse — for the rep, during the call

MeetMouse listens through your Mac (no bot — the buyer sees nothing), transcribes on-device, and applies a coaching rubric to the live conversation. When the buyer raises a concern and you roll past it, a cue appears: "CFO cost worry just came up. Pause the roadmap and circle back to it." It watches talk balance, buried asks, and vague "sounds good" non-commitments. The overlay is excluded from screen shares, so you can demo with it running.

After the call: notes with next steps and owners, and a chat that answers "what did they say about budget?" with the transcript timestamp. What it doesn't do: CRM sync, manager dashboards, team call libraries. $20 once, and the rubric is yours to edit.

2. Gong — the sales org's system of record

If you have a RevOps team and a real budget, Gong is the category leader: call recording, deal risk, forecasting, and a library managers mine for coaching. Priced for enterprises and sold through a sales process. The coaching happens in review sessions after the fact.

3. Fireflies — Gong-lite for smaller teams

Records, transcribes, and pushes to your CRM, with conversation analytics at a fraction of Gong's price (from $18/user/month). A bot joins every call and everything lives in their cloud.

4. Fathom — the best free notes for sellers

Fathom's free plan gives reps great call summaries and it can fill CRM fields on paid tiers. A bot joins the call — some buyers don't care, some go quiet.

5. Granola — bot-free notes

If the bot is a problem with your buyers (it often is in enterprise and procurement calls), Granola captures audio without joining. Cloud AI, $14/user/month after the free 25 notes.

Why live beats recap for individual reps

A recap tells you that you talked 70% of the call and never asked for next steps. By then the buyer has hung up. Live cues are the only format that changes this deal. The best setup for a lot of teams: the org keeps its recorder for the CRM, and reps run a private live coach alongside it.

So which one?

  • Enterprise sales org with RevOps: Gong.
  • Small team that needs CRM plumbing: Fireflies or Fathom Team.
  • Buyer-facing calls where a bot hurts: Granola for notes.
  • You, winning the call you're on right now: MeetMouse, $20 once.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI tool for sales calls?

For sales orgs, Gong (enterprise) or Fireflies (smaller budgets) for recording, analytics, and CRM sync. For an individual rep who wants live coaching during the call — catching buried objections and missed asks — MeetMouse, $20 once.

Is there real-time AI coaching for sales calls?

Yes. MeetMouse shows short cues during the call in an overlay only you see (it's hidden from screen shares). Most sales AI, including Gong, Fireflies, and Fathom, coaches after the call from a recording.

Will the buyer know I'm using an AI assistant?

With bot-based tools (Gong, Fireflies, Fathom), a recorder joins the meeting and is visible. MeetMouse and Granola capture audio on your Mac, so no participant joins. Follow recording-consent rules where you operate either way.

Does MeetMouse integrate with Salesforce or HubSpot?

No. MeetMouse keeps meetings on your Mac and has no CRM sync. Many reps run it alongside their team's recorder: the recorder feeds the CRM, MeetMouse coaches them live.
