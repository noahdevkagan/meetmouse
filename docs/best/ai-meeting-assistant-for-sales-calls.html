The best AI meeting assistant for sales calls

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: Sales org with a real budget? Gong. Small team that needs notes in the CRM? Fireflies or Fathom. Buyers hate the bot? Granola. Want to catch the objection before the call ends instead of reading about it after? That's MeetMouse — $20 once.

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse. I've also done a ton of sales calls — AppSumo deals, partnerships, sponsors — and here's what I learned: I never lost a deal in the recap. I lost it at minute nine, when the buyer said "I'd have to run this by finance" and I kept pitching features.

That's the whole split in this category. Almost every sales AI works after the call: record, summarize, push to the CRM, let a manager review it next week. Great for the org. Almost none work during the call, when you can still save it.

1. Gong — if you have RevOps and a budget

Gong is the category leader: recordings, deal risk, forecasting, a call library managers mine for coaching. Priced for enterprises, sold through a sales process (respect). The coaching happens in review sessions — after.

2. Fireflies — Gong-lite

Records, transcribes, scores, and pushes to the CRM for a fraction of Gong. From $18/user/month. A bot joins every call. My comparison.

3. Fathom — free notes that fill in the CRM

If you're a rep who just wants great call notes for free, stop reading and get Fathom. The bot is visible; some buyers don't care, some go quiet.

4. Granola — when the bot kills the vibe

Enterprise and procurement calls get weird when a recorder joins. Granola hears the call through your computer instead. Cloud notes, $14/user/month after 25.

5. MeetMouse — for the rep, during the call

Mine. It listens through your Mac — the buyer sees nothing — and watches the conversation live. When they raise a concern and you steamroll past it, you get a cue: "CFO cost worry just came up. Pause the roadmap and circle back to it." It also flags when you've been talking too long and when "sounds good" wasn't actually a next step. The overlay is hidden from screen shares, so you can demo with it running.

After the call you get notes with next steps and owners. The coaching rubric is editable, so you can bake in your own discovery questions. No CRM sync — it's for you, not your manager. Run it next to the team's recorder: one feeds the CRM, the other helps you close. $20 once. If it doesn't change how your meetings go, email me within 30 days and I'll refund you.

So which one?

  • Enterprise sales org: Gong.
  • Small team that lives in the CRM: Fireflies or Fathom.
  • Buyers who freeze when a bot joins: Granola.
  • You, winning the call you're on right now: MeetMouse.

Frequently asked questions

What's the best AI tool for sales calls?

For sales orgs, Gong (enterprise) or Fireflies (smaller budgets). For free notes, Fathom. For live coaching during the call — catching buried objections and missed asks — MeetMouse, $20 once.

Is there real-time AI coaching for sales calls?

Yes. MeetMouse shows short cues during the call in an overlay only you see. Most sales AI, including Gong, Fireflies, and Fathom, coaches after the call from a recording.

Will the buyer know I'm using AI?

With Gong, Fireflies, and Fathom, a recorder joins the call. MeetMouse and Granola capture audio on your computer, so nothing joins. Recording-consent laws still apply to you either way.

Does MeetMouse sync to Salesforce or HubSpot?

No. It keeps meetings on your Mac. Plenty of reps run it alongside the team's recorder — the recorder feeds the CRM, MeetMouse coaches them live.
