The best AI notetaker for Zoom

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: If you host on a paid Zoom plan, try Zoom AI Companion first — it's included. For free notes in anyone's Zoom, Fathom (a bot joins). For no bot at all, Granola (cloud) or MeetMouse (on your Mac, with live coaching, $20 once).

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse. Zoom is where the bot question gets most annoying: hosts can block unknown participants, waiting rooms hold bots hostage, and in someone else's meeting you often can't turn on Zoom's own AI at all.

So the right Zoom notetaker depends on one question: whose meeting is it?

1. Zoom AI Companion — if it's your meeting

If you host on a paid Zoom account, AI Companion summaries are already included and need no bot. The catches: it's Zoom-only, the host controls it, and data lives in Zoom's cloud.

2. Fathom — free in any Zoom meeting

Fathom's bot joins any Zoom you're in (if the host admits it) and produces excellent free summaries. Visible to everyone.

3. Granola — bot-free, cross-platform

Granola hears Zoom through your computer's audio, so nothing joins. Cloud notes; subscription after 25 notes.

4. MeetMouse — bot-free, on-device, and live

MeetMouse also listens through your Mac, so it works in any Zoom — yours, a client's, a webinar — with no participant and nothing for the host to block. It's the only one here that coaches you during the Zoom: cues in an overlay that stays out of your screen share. Transcription and AI run on your Mac, and you get notes and a chat afterward.

It's Mac-only and doesn't use Zoom's APIs, so it doesn't know participant names from Zoom — speakers are separated by voice. $20 once.

5. Otter — for the archive

Otter's live transcript and searchable history are strong for recurring Zoom meetings, with a bot joining each one.

So which one?

  • You host, paid Zoom plan: Zoom AI Companion.
  • Free, any meeting, bot is fine: Fathom.
  • No bot, cloud notes: Granola.
  • No bot, nothing uploaded, live coaching: MeetMouse.

Frequently asked questions

What's the best AI notetaker for Zoom?

If you host on a paid plan, Zoom AI Companion is included. For any meeting, Fathom is free (bot joins), Granola is bot-free with cloud notes, and MeetMouse is bot-free, on-device, and coaches you live for $20 once.

Can I use an AI notetaker in a Zoom meeting I don't host?

Bot-based tools need the host to admit them. Bot-free apps like MeetMouse and Granola capture audio on your computer, so they work in any meeting you can hear.

Is there a Zoom notetaker without a bot?

Yes: Zoom AI Companion (host-controlled), Granola, and MeetMouse. MeetMouse is the only one that keeps everything on your device.

Does the MeetMouse overlay show up when I share my screen in Zoom?

No. The overlay is excluded from screen sharing and recordings, so only you see the cues.
