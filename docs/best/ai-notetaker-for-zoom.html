1. Zoom AI Companion — if it's your meeting

If you host on a paid Zoom account, you're already paying for AI Companion and it needs no bot. The catches: Zoom only, the host controls it, and your data sits in Zoom's cloud. Use what you've got first. MeetMouse vs Zoom AI Companion.

2. Fathom — free in anybody's Zoom

Joins any meeting the host lets it into and writes excellent summaries. Free. Everybody sees it.

3. Otter — for recurring meetings you'll search later

Live transcript and a searchable history. Bot joins each one.

4. Granola — no bot, cloud notes

Hears Zoom through your computer, so nothing joins and nothing gets stuck in a waiting room. Subscription after 25 notes.

5. MeetMouse — no bot, nothing uploaded, and it helps live

Mine. It listens through your Mac, so it works in any Zoom — yours, a client's, a webinar — with nothing for the host to block. It's the only one here that coaches you during the call, in an overlay that stays out of your screen share. Notes and a chat after. Everything runs on your Mac.

It doesn't plug into Zoom's API, so it doesn't pull participant names from Zoom — it tells speakers apart by voice. Mac only. $20 once. If it doesn't change how your meetings go, email me within 30 days and I'll refund you.

Frequently asked questions

What's the best AI notetaker for Zoom?

If you host on a paid plan, Zoom AI Companion is included. For any meeting: Fathom is free (bot joins), Granola is bot-free with cloud notes, and MeetMouse is bot-free, on-device, and coaches you live for $20 once.

Can I use an AI notetaker in a Zoom I don't host?

Bot tools need the host to let them in. Bot-free apps like MeetMouse and Granola capture audio on your computer, so they work in any meeting you can hear.

Is there a Zoom notetaker without a bot?

Yes — Zoom AI Companion (host-controlled), Granola, and MeetMouse. MeetMouse is the only one that keeps everything on your device.

No. It's excluded from screen shares and recordings.
