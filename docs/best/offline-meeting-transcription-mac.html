The best offline meeting transcription for Mac

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: For live meetings — transcription, notes, and coaching with Wi-Fi off — MeetMouse, $20 once. For transcribing recordings after the fact, MacWhisper. Granola, Otter, Fathom, and Fireflies all need the internet.

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse. "Works offline" is the easiest claim in this category to test, so test it: start a meeting, turn off Wi-Fi, keep talking. Most "private" tools stop, because the transcription or the summary happens on a server.

On Apple Silicon, on-device speech recognition is now good enough for real meetings. The differences are in what else the app does locally (summaries, Q&A, speaker labels) and whether it handles live calls or only files.

1. MeetMouse — live, offline, end to end

MeetMouse captures system audio plus your mic, transcribes on-device (with speaker separation on Apple Silicon), and runs a local AI model for live coaching cues and post-meeting notes. After a one-time model download, it needs no network at all. On a plane, in a SCIF-adjacent office, on hotel Wi-Fi you don't trust — same behavior.

Honest limits: Apple Silicon recommended (Intel Macs use Apple's built-in transcription with lower accuracy), local AI models want spare memory, and there's no cloud sync by design.

2. MacWhisper — the best for files

If you record interviews or lectures and want the most accurate offline text, MacWhisper with a large Whisper model is excellent. Not a live meeting assistant.

3. Apple's built-in tools — free and fine for simple cases

Voice Memos and dictation use on-device speech models on modern Macs. Good enough for a quick record; no meeting structure, speakers, or summaries.

Why the cloud tools can't do this

Granola, Otter, Fathom, and Fireflies all generate transcripts or notes on their servers. That's not a flaw — it's how they offer cross-device sync and big models — but it means no offline mode.

So which one?

  • Live meetings, fully offline: MeetMouse.
  • Recorded files: MacWhisper.
  • Quick free recording: Voice Memos.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a meeting transcription app for Mac that works offline?

Yes. MeetMouse transcribes live meetings and generates notes on your Mac with Wi-Fi off, after a one-time model download. MacWhisper transcribes recordings offline.

Does Granola work offline?

No. Granola generates notes with cloud AI, so it needs an internet connection.

How accurate is on-device transcription?

On Apple Silicon, modern on-device models are accurate enough for meetings and include speaker separation in MeetMouse. Intel Macs fall back to Apple's built-in engine with lower accuracy.

How much RAM do I need for local meeting AI?

Transcription runs comfortably on any Apple Silicon Mac. Local AI models need more memory; MeetMouse checks what's available and picks accordingly.
