What an AI meeting coach actually does

Most meeting tools are notetakers. They tell you what happened after it's over. A coach tries to change what happens, and the timing matters a lot. A cue that says "you've been talking for four minutes" is useful at minute four. In a recap the next day it's trivia.

So the first question isn't which app. It's whether you want feedback during the call, after it, or before it.

What MeetMouse coaches out of the box

Out of the box MeetMouse watches for five things, on purpose. More cues sounds better until you're getting pinged every ninety seconds. The defaults:

Long stretches. You've been talking a long time in one go. Stacked questions. You asked three things at once, so they'll answer the easiest. No next steps. The meeting is nearly over and nobody has said what happens next. Soft commitments. Someone said "I'll try to get to it" and no date got pinned. And one green: when you lock an owner and a date out loud, it tells you that landed.

About twenty more cues are off by default and one switch away in Coaching Style: interruptions, unanswered questions, the other person going quiet, a worry that got passed over, time checks. Cues show in a small overlay that's hidden from screen shares and recordings, so the other people on the call never see it.

Questions

What is an AI meeting coach?
Software that gives you feedback on how you run meetings. Live coaches nudge you during the call; post-call coaches score the recording afterwards; practice coaches let you rehearse with an AI first.
Is MeetMouse the same as MeetingCoach?
Yes. MeetingCoach was renamed MeetMouse in September 2026. Same app, same team, and existing installs updated automatically.
Is there a free AI meeting coach?
MeetMouse is free: unlimited your first week, then 3 meetings a week, with notes never locked. Unlimited is $20 once. Yoodli has a free plan for practice roleplays.
Can other people on the call see the coaching?
Not with MeetMouse. Cues show in an overlay that's excluded from screen shares and recordings, and no bot joins the call.
Does an AI meeting coach record my calls?
Depends on the tool. Bot-based tools record to their cloud. MeetMouse transcribes on your Mac and keeps the meeting there by default; if you add your own Claude or OpenAI key, meeting text goes to that provider.
Which meetings does MeetMouse work with?
Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and other calls on your Mac, because it listens to your Mac's audio instead of joining the call.
