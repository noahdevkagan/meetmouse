Questions

Does audio or text ever leave my Mac?
Audio never does. By default transcription and the AI model both run locally, and the only network requests are the one-time model download and signed update checks. If you choose to add a Claude or OpenAI key in Settings, meeting text goes directly to that provider and nowhere else.
Does a bot join my call?
No. MeetMouse listens to your Mac's audio, so there is no extra participant, no recording notice, and nothing for the other side to see.
What do I need to run it?
A Mac on macOS 14.2 or later — Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) recommended. On Intel Macs the high-accuracy transcription engine and speaker identification aren't available, so sessions run on Apple's built-in transcription with reduced accuracy. The AI runtime is bundled — nothing else to install.
Which meeting apps does it work with?
Anything that plays through your Mac — Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, FaceTime, Slack huddles, a browser tab. MeetMouse needs Microphone and Screen Recording (for system audio) permissions.
Is there a free trial?
There is a 30-day money-back guarantee instead. Buy it, use it in real meetings, and if it hasn't earned its keep, email me and I'll refund you.
