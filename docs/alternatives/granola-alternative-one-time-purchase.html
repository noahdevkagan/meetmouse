MeetMouse — $20, then it's yours

No bot (captures your Mac's audio like Granola), on-device transcription, a local AI model for notes with owners, a chat over each meeting, and live coaching cues during the call. No account to create or cancel. Updates are included, delivered through signed auto-updates.

Why can it be one-time? Because it runs on your Mac. Cloud notetakers pay for servers and AI tokens on every meeting you record, forever — so they have to charge you forever. MeetMouse's compute is your laptop.

Fathom — $0, with a bot

If the goal is simply to stop paying, Fathom's free plan has no note cap. You accept a visible bot and cloud storage.

MacWhisper — one-time, for recordings

A one-time Pro license gets you excellent offline transcription of audio files. Not a live meeting assistant.

What you give up leaving Granola

Cross-platform apps (Windows, iPhone), shared team folders, and cloud sync. If you rely on those, a subscription is paying for something real.

So which one?

  • Pay once, keep it private: MeetMouse, $20.
  • Pay nothing, accept a bot: Fathom.
  • Need team folders and Windows: stay on Granola.
