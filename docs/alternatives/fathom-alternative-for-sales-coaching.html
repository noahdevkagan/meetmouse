A Fathom alternative that coaches you during the sale

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: Fathom gives reps excellent free call summaries — after the call. For coaching during it (the objection you skipped, the next step you didn't ask for), MeetMouse shows live cues with no bot and nothing uploaded, $20 once. For org-wide review coaching, Gong.

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse. Fathom is where a lot of reps start, and for good reason: free, accurate summaries, CRM fields filled in. But the "coaching" in most call tools means a manager reviewing a recording next week.

By then the deal is already shaped. The single best moment to coach a rep is the second after the buyer says "we'd need to get legal involved" — while the rep can still ask "what would legal need to see?"

How it works

MeetMouse captures the call through your Mac (no bot — the buyer sees nothing), transcribes on-device, and applies an editable coaching rubric to the live conversation. Cues appear in an overlay excluded from screen shares, so you can demo with it running. After the call: notes with next steps and owners. The rubric is yours — add your methodology or discovery questions.

Run it alongside Fathom

MeetMouse doesn't sync to CRMs, and it doesn't try to. Many reps keep Fathom (or the team's recorder) for the CRM and run MeetMouse for themselves. One documents the deal; the other helps win it.

Gong for org-level coaching

If you're a sales leader who needs deal analytics and review workflows across a team, Gong is the enterprise standard.

So which one?

  • Free summaries and CRM fields: Fathom.
  • Live coaching for the rep on the call: MeetMouse.
  • Org-wide analytics and review coaching: Gong.

Frequently asked questions

Does Fathom provide real-time sales coaching?

Fathom focuses on notes, summaries, and CRM updates after the call. For live cues during the call, you need a real-time coach like MeetMouse.

What is real-time sales coaching software?

Tools that analyze the conversation as it happens and prompt the rep — for example, to address an objection or ask for next steps. MeetMouse does this in a private overlay, on-device.

Can I use MeetMouse and Fathom together?

Yes. Fathom's bot records for the CRM; MeetMouse listens through your Mac and coaches you live. They don't conflict.

Will the buyer see the coaching?

No. MeetMouse's overlay is excluded from screen shares and recordings, and no bot joins the call.
