A Granola alternative for sales calls

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: Granola is a great notetaker, but sales calls are won during the call. MeetMouse keeps the no-bot capture and adds live cues — buried objections, talk balance, missed asks — on your Mac, $20 once. Need CRM sync? Fireflies or Fathom. Enterprise? Gong.

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse. Lots of reps use Granola because it doesn't put a bot in front of buyers. Smart. But look at what you actually do with the notes: you read, after the call, that the buyer mentioned their CFO twice and you never addressed it.

For sales, the useful moment is minute nine, not minute forty-five.

MeetMouse — the bot-free sales coach

Same invisible capture as Granola, so buyers see nothing. While you sell, MeetMouse applies a rubric to the live conversation and shows a short cue in an overlay that doesn't appear in your screen share — so you can demo with it running. Typical cues: "They raised budget; you haven't answered it." "You've talked for 3 minutes. Ask a question." "'Sounds good' isn't a next step — propose a date."

After the call you get notes with next steps and owners, and a chat to ask "what did they say about timeline?" The rubric is editable — drop in your MEDDICC or discovery framework. No CRM sync; conversations stay on your Mac.

Fireflies or Fathom — if the CRM is the point

If you need call notes landing in Salesforce or HubSpot automatically, a bot-based recorder is the practical choice. Plenty of reps run one for the CRM and MeetMouse for the live coaching.

Gong — the enterprise stack

Revenue intelligence, forecasting, and manager coaching libraries, priced for enterprises.

So which one?

  • Win the call you're on, no bot: MeetMouse.
  • Automatic CRM notes: Fireflies or Fathom.
  • Enterprise revenue intelligence: Gong.

Frequently asked questions

Is Granola good for sales calls?

Granola is good for bot-free notes on sales calls, but it only helps after the call. It doesn't coach you live or deeply sync to CRMs.

What's a Granola alternative with live sales coaching?

MeetMouse. It's bot-free like Granola and shows live cues during the call — buried objections, talk balance, missed next steps — processed on your Mac.

Can I use my own sales methodology?

Yes. MeetMouse's coaching rubric is editable, so you can add your discovery framework or methodology.

Will buyers see MeetMouse when I share my screen?

No. The overlay is excluded from screen shares and recordings.
