A Fathom alternative without a bot

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: Bot-free options: Granola (polished cloud notes, subscription), MeetMouse (on your Mac, nothing uploaded, live coaching, $20 once), Krisp (notes plus noise cancellation), and your platform's built-in AI if you host.

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse. Fathom is excellent and free. Its one unavoidable trait: a participant joins your meeting. In internal standups nobody cares. In a candidate interview, a skittish enterprise call, a sensitive 1:1, or a client's meeting where IT blocks bots — it changes the room, or doesn't get in at all.

Bot-free tools work differently: they capture the audio on your own computer, the same way you hear the call. No participant, no "recording" banner from the tool, nothing for the host to admit.

1. MeetMouse — no bot, no cloud

MeetMouse hears the call through your Mac's audio in any app: Zoom, Meet, Teams, FaceTime, Slack huddles, a browser tab. Nothing joins. Transcription and the default AI model run on-device, so there's also no vendor in the loop. After the call: notes with next steps and owners, and a chat over the transcript.

Unlike Fathom (or any notetaker), it also coaches you live in an overlay that stays out of your screen share. Mac only. $20 once.

2. Granola — no bot, polished notes

The best-known bot-free notetaker. Beautiful notes, team folders, Mac/Windows/iPhone. Cloud AI; the free plan caps history at 25 notes.

3. Krisp — no bot, plus noise cancellation

Useful if you already want Krisp's noise cancellation. Notes are cloud-processed.

4. Built-in platform AI — if you host

Zoom AI Companion, Google Meet's Gemini notes, and Teams Copilot need no bot, but only work in meetings where the host has them enabled, and only on that platform.

So which one?

  • Polished bot-free notes, cloud is fine: Granola.
  • No bot and nothing uploaded, plus live coaching: MeetMouse.
  • You host everything on one platform: its built-in AI.

Frequently asked questions

Can Fathom work without joining as a bot?

Fathom's notetaker joins meetings as a participant. For no participant at all, use a tool that captures audio on your computer, like MeetMouse or Granola.

What's the best bot-free alternative to Fathom?

Granola for polished cloud notes; MeetMouse for fully on-device notes with live coaching, $20 once.

Do bot-free notetakers work in meetings I don't host?

Yes. Because they capture your computer's audio, they work in any meeting you can hear, regardless of host settings. Follow consent laws where you are.

Will other participants know I'm using MeetMouse?

No participant joins and the overlay is hidden from screen shares. It's still good practice to tell people you take AI notes.
