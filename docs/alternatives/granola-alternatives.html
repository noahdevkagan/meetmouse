Granola alternatives: what to use instead (from a guy who competes with them)

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: Leaving over price and don't mind a bot? Fathom (free). Want it private — nothing in anyone's cloud — or want to get better at meetings, not just remember them? MeetMouse, $20 once, on your Mac. Need a searchable team archive? Otter. Sales org? Fireflies.

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse. I've said in my Granola comparison that Granola is the best bot-free notetaker around, and I stand by it. It's well-designed, it doesn't put a bot in your call, and its notes are great.

So why look elsewhere? Usually one of four reasons: the cloud (your notes are generated on their servers), the subscription ($14/user/month after the free 25 notes), platform or team fit, or the ceiling — beautiful notes about meetings that still go badly. Here's who fixes which.

1. MeetMouse — the private, no-subscription Granola alternative

Same no-bot approach: MeetMouse listens through your Mac's audio. The difference is where things go. Transcription runs on-device and the default AI model runs locally, so meetings never touch anyone's servers — it works with Wi-Fi off. You still get topic-by-topic notes with owners after the call, a chat that answers questions with transcript timestamps, and encrypted, expiring share links for the notes.

And one thing Granola doesn't do: MeetMouse coaches you during the meeting. A short cue in an overlay (hidden from screen shares) when you're monologuing, skipped the ask, or rolled past a concern. $20 once, no account. Trade-offs: Mac only, no team folders, no Windows or iPhone app.

2. Fathom — if the problem is the price

Fathom's free plan is the most generous in the category, with no note cap. The trade is a visible bot in every meeting and cloud storage. Granola vs Fathom.

3. Otter — if you want an archive

Otter's strength is years of searchable transcripts and a live transcript during calls. Bot-based; free tier stops at 300 minutes/month. Granola vs Otter.

4. Fireflies — if you're a sales team

CRM sync, call analytics, team dashboards. From $18/user/month, bot and cloud. Granola vs Fireflies.

5. tl;dv — if you share moments

Clip the 40 seconds that matter and send them to the team. Free recordings delete after 3 months; Pro $29/month.

6. Krisp — bot-free notes plus noise cancellation

Krisp started as noise cancellation and added bot-free AI meeting notes. Handy if you want both in one app; notes are processed in their cloud.

7. The $0 route

Voice Memos or QuickTime, paste into ChatGPT or Claude, ask for action items. No subscription, fully manual.

So which one?

  • Free, and a bot is fine: Fathom.
  • Searchable archive: Otter.
  • Sales team with a CRM: Fireflies.
  • Private (nothing uploaded), no subscription, and live coaching: MeetMouse, $20 once.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to Granola?

It depends on why you're switching. For free notes, Fathom. For privacy and no subscription, MeetMouse ($20 once, on-device, no bot). For a searchable archive, Otter. For sales teams, Fireflies.

Is there a Granola alternative that doesn't use the cloud?

Yes. MeetMouse transcribes and summarizes on your Mac with a local AI model by default, and works with Wi-Fi off. Granola generates notes with cloud AI.

Is there a Granola alternative without a subscription?

MeetMouse is $20 one time with no subscription or account. Fathom has a free plan but uses a bot and charges for team features.

Does Granola join the call as a bot?

No. Granola captures your computer's audio, so no participant joins. MeetMouse works the same way; the difference is that MeetMouse processes the conversation on your Mac instead of in the cloud.
