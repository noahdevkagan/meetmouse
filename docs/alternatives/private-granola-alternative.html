A private Granola alternative: no bot and no cloud

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: Granola solved the bot, not the cloud — your meeting content still goes to their servers and AI providers. MeetMouse keeps the same no-bot capture but runs transcription and AI on your Mac, works with Wi-Fi off, and costs $20 once.

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse. A lot of people pick Granola because it feels private — there's no bot, nobody on the call knows. That's real privacy from the other participants. It isn't privacy from the vendor: Granola generates notes with cloud AI, which means transcript-derived content leaves your machine.

For most meetings, that's fine. For client calls under NDA, legal, health, HR, M&A, fundraising, or anything you'd hesitate to email to a stranger, it's the whole question.

How MeetMouse keeps it local

MeetMouse captures system audio and your mic through macOS (the same permission Granola uses), transcribes on-device, and runs an embedded AI model bound to your own machine. The only network requests in local mode are the one-time model download and signed update checks. No account, no telemetry.

You still get what you liked about Granola: notes after every call with topics, decisions, and owners, plus a chat over each saved meeting that cites transcript timestamps. If you want to share, one click makes an encrypted, expiring link with just the curated notes — never the transcript.

What you give up

  • Other platforms. MeetMouse is Mac only (macOS 14.2+, Apple Silicon recommended). Granola has Windows and iPhone.
  • Team workspaces. No shared folders; it's a personal tool.
  • The biggest cloud models by default. Local models are good, not frontier. You can opt in to your own Claude or OpenAI key — then meeting text goes to that provider.

Other private options

MacWhisper transcribes recordings locally but isn't a live meeting assistant. The scrappy route — record with Voice Memos, summarize with a local model — works if you're technical and patient.

So which one?

  • Team notes, cloud is fine: stay on Granola.
  • Nothing leaves your Mac: MeetMouse.
  • Offline transcription of recordings: MacWhisper.

Frequently asked questions

Is Granola private?

Granola doesn't put a bot in your meeting, so other participants don't see it. But it generates notes with cloud AI, so meeting content is processed on its servers and by AI providers.

Is there a Granola alternative that runs locally?

Yes. MeetMouse transcribes on your Mac and uses a local AI model by default. It works with Wi-Fi off and requires no account.

Does Granola work offline?

No. It needs an internet connection to generate notes.

Can I get Granola-style notes without the cloud?

MeetMouse produces topic-by-topic notes with next steps and owners after each call, plus a chat over the transcript — all on-device by default.
