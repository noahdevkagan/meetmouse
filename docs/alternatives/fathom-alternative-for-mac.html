A Fathom alternative built for the Mac

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: Fathom works through a bot, so it's the same on every computer. Native Mac apps capture audio from any app — FaceTime, Slack huddles, WhatsApp calls — with no bot. MeetMouse does it all on-device with live coaching ($20 once). Granola does it with cloud notes.

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse, a native Mac app. Fathom's bot-based approach is platform-neutral, which is great for teams on mixed machines. But it only works where a bot can join: Zoom, Meet, Teams. Your FaceTime with a client, the Slack huddle, the phone call relayed through your Mac — Fathom can't hear those.

A native Mac app uses macOS's system-audio capture instead. If you can hear it, it can transcribe it.

MeetMouse — made only for Mac

MeetMouse is Mac-only on purpose: it's built on ScreenCaptureKit for system audio, on-device speech models tuned for Apple Silicon, and an embedded local AI model. It works with Zoom, Meet, Teams, FaceTime, Slack huddles, and browser calls. It can even detect when a meeting starts and offer to begin.

During the call, a small overlay (hidden from screen shares) coaches you live. After the call, notes and a chat. It lives in your menu bar, and nothing leaves your Mac by default. Requires macOS 14.2+; Apple Silicon recommended.

Granola — bot-free, cross-platform

Also native on Mac and captures any app. Cloud notes, subscription after 25 notes, plus Windows and iPhone apps if you need them.

When Fathom is still the right answer

If your team is on mixed Windows/Mac machines and wants one shared library of recordings for free, Fathom's bot approach is simpler.

So which one?

  • Mac-native, private, with live coaching: MeetMouse.
  • Mac-native with cloud team notes: Granola.
  • Mixed-OS team, free: Fathom.

Frequently asked questions

Does Fathom have a Mac app?

Fathom works mainly through a bot that joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams calls, so it behaves the same on any computer. It can't capture apps where a bot can't join, like FaceTime.

What's the best Fathom alternative for Mac?

MeetMouse for on-device notes and live coaching ($20 once), or Granola for bot-free cloud notes.

Can a Mac notetaker transcribe FaceTime calls?

Yes, native apps like MeetMouse capture system audio, so they work with FaceTime, Slack huddles, and any other app. Follow consent laws where you are.

What Mac do I need for MeetMouse?

macOS 14.2 or later. Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) is recommended; Intel Macs use Apple's built-in transcription with reduced accuracy.
