A private Fathom alternative: your meetings stay on your Mac

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: Fathom records and stores your meetings in its cloud, with a bot in the call. MeetMouse captures without a bot, transcribes and summarizes on your Mac, keeps no account or server copy, and works with Wi-Fi off. $20 once.

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse. Fathom is great free software. It's free partly because it's built as a cloud service: your recordings, transcripts, and summaries live on its servers so it can show them to you (and your team) anywhere.

If you're asking "who can access my meetings?", the most private answer is: nobody, because they never left your computer.

How MeetMouse handles your data

Audio never leaves your Mac, in any mode. By default transcription and the AI model both run locally; the only network requests are the one-time model download and signed update checks. There's zero telemetry. Transcripts and notes are saved on your Mac.

If you choose to add your own Claude or OpenAI key for a bigger model, meeting text goes directly to that provider and nowhere else. Leave it on local and nothing goes anywhere.

What you keep from Fathom

Notes after every call with topics and next steps with owners; a chat that answers questions about a meeting with timestamps; easy sharing of the notes. Plus live coaching during the call, which Fathom doesn't do.

What you give up

Video recordings and clips, team libraries, CRM sync, and a free price. MeetMouse is a Mac-only personal tool.

So which one?

  • Free team notes with recordings: stay on Fathom.
  • Meetings that must stay private: MeetMouse.

Frequently asked questions

Is Fathom private?

Fathom stores recordings and transcripts in its cloud and joins meetings as a visible bot. It's a reputable service, but your meetings live on its servers.

What's the most private alternative to Fathom?

MeetMouse: no bot, no account, transcription and AI on your Mac by default, and it works with Wi-Fi off.

Does MeetMouse record video?

No. It works from audio and keeps transcripts and notes locally. There's no video recording or clip library.

Can I share notes from a private notetaker?

Yes. MeetMouse creates an encrypted, expiring link containing only the curated notes, and you can revoke it anytime.
