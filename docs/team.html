One private page for your team's meeting notes

Everyone takes notes on their own Mac with MeetMouse. The notes each person chooses to share land on one team page, newest first and searchable.

$40 once. Unlimited seats. No subscription, no accounts.

How it works

  1. One person buys the license and creates the team in MeetMouse (Settings → Team).
  2. They send the invite link to the team. Joining unlocks unlimited meetings on each teammate's Mac.
  3. After a meeting, click Share with team. The notes show up on the team page for everyone.

What gets shared

  • Only the notes: summary, topics and next steps, with the author's name.
  • Never the transcript, the audio or the coaching. Those stay on the Mac that recorded them.
  • Nothing is shared automatically. Each note goes up when its owner clicks the button.
  • Notes are encrypted on the Mac. The key is in your team's invite link, so MeetMouse can't read them. Anyone with the link can read and add notes, so send it only to your team.
  • Notes stay on the team page for a year. The original always stays on the author's Mac.

MeetMouse runs on Macs (macOS 14.2 or later). The team page opens in any browser. Questions? support@meetmouse.com
