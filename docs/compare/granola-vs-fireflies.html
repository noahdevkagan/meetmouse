Granola vs Fireflies: notes for you, or a machine for your team?

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: Granola is for individuals and small teams who want great notes with no bot in the call — $14/user/month after 25 free notes. Fireflies is for sales and ops teams who need every call recorded, searchable, and pushed into the CRM — from $18/user/month, with a bot. Both are cloud. If you want no bot and no cloud, see the end.

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse, which competes with both. I'll give you the straight comparison first.

These two look similar on a feature grid and feel completely different in use. Granola is a quiet notebook that happens to hear the meeting. Fireflies is infrastructure: a recorder that joins, a database of every call, and integrations into your stack.

Where Granola wins

  • No bot. Buyers, candidates, and executives don't see a recorder join.
  • Note quality. Blending your own typed notes with AI makes for summaries that read like yours.
  • Simplicity. Nothing to configure.

Where Fireflies wins

  • CRM and workflow automation. Notes and fields flow into your tools.
  • Recordings and search. Replay the actual audio, search across the team.
  • Manager visibility. Conversation analytics across reps.

Frequently asked questions

Is Granola or Fireflies better?

Granola is better for individuals who want bot-free notes. Fireflies is better for teams that need recordings, analytics, and CRM integrations.

Does Fireflies join meetings as a bot?

Yes, Fireflies' notetaker joins as a participant. Granola doesn't — it captures your computer's audio.

Which is cheaper, Granola or Fireflies?

Granola has a free plan capped at 25 notes and Business at $14/user/month. Fireflies paid plans start around $18/user/month. MeetMouse is $20 once.

Is there an alternative to both that doesn't use the cloud?

MeetMouse is bot-free and runs transcription and AI on your Mac by default, so nothing is uploaded.
