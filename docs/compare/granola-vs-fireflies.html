Granola vs Fireflies: a notebook vs a machine

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: Granola if you're one person (or a small team) who wants great notes and no bot in the call. Fireflies if you run a sales team that needs every call recorded, scored, and pushed into the CRM. They look similar on a feature grid and feel nothing alike in real life.

Quick disclosure: I make MeetMouse, which competes with both of these sideways — it coaches you live instead of just taking notes. I don't win by trashing either one, so here's the comparison I'd want if I were choosing.

What each tool is

Granola sits on your computer, hears the call through your system audio (no bot), and blends what you type with an AI summary after. It's a personal notebook that happens to hear the meeting.

Fireflies sends a bot into the call, records it, and turns every conversation into data: searchable transcripts, talk-time stats, scorecards, CRM fields. It's infrastructure for a team.

Where Granola wins

  • No bot. No "Fireflies.ai Notetaker has joined" moment. Buyers and candidates don't stiffen up.
  • Note quality. Notes read like a sharp human wrote them, because one half-did.
  • Simplicity. Install it, it works. Fireflies is a truck when most people need a bicycle.

Where Fireflies wins

  • The CRM machine. Calls land in Salesforce or HubSpot without anyone typing.
  • Recordings and search. Replay the actual audio; search every call the team ever had.
  • Managers. Talk-time and scorecards across reps. If you run a sales floor, this is why you buy it.

The part neither fixes

Both send your meetings to the cloud, and both work after the call's already over. The summary of the call where you talked over the buyer is still a call where you talked over the buyer. Frequently asked questions

Is Granola better than Fireflies?

For one person who wants bot-free notes, yes. For a sales team that needs recordings, analytics, and CRM sync, no — that's Fireflies' home turf.

Yes. Fireflies' notetaker joins as a visible participant. Granola doesn't — it captures your computer's audio.
