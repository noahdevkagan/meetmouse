MeetMouse vs Fathom: the best free notetaker vs a live coach

Disclosure: MeetMouse is mine. And this page is going to read weirdly positive about a "competitor," because Fathom earned it — their free plan is unlimited recordings, summaries, and action items with no meaningful catch. When a free thing is good, say so.

The short answer

If you want free meeting notes, use Fathom — it's the best free notetaker on the market and I'm not going to pretend otherwise. MeetMouse doesn't compete on notes. It coaches you during the call, entirely on your Mac, for $20 once. Different problems. A lot of people should run both.

What each tool actually does

Fathom sends a bot into your Zoom, Meet, or Teams call, records everything, and hands you polished summaries and action items afterward. Free, unlimited. It's the tool I recommend first when someone says "I just want notes and I don't want to pay."

MeetMouse works the other side of the meeting. No bot, no recording archive — it runs on your Mac, listens live, and shows you short coaching cues in an overlay while the conversation is still happening: you're monologuing, you never made the ask, that answer was a dodge. The overlay is invisible in screen shares, and everything — transcription and AI — runs on-device. WiFi off? Still works.

Where Fathom wins

  • Price for notes: free beats everything. If notes are the job, you cannot out-value "unlimited and $0." Nobody can, including me.
  • The recording archive. Rewatch calls, clip highlights, share summaries with the team. MeetMouse keeps no cloud archive at all.
  • Cross-platform. Windows users exist. MeetMouse is Mac-only.

Where MeetMouse wins

  • Timing. Fathom's summary arrives after your fate is sealed. If you talked 80% of the call, the summary just formalizes it. MeetMouse taps you at minute two, when the meeting is still winnable.
  • No bot, no vibe shift. Recording bots make candid conversations less candid. Ask anyone in sales or negotiations. MeetMouse's presence is undetectable by design.
  • Privacy that doesn't depend on a policy doc. Free cloud products pay their bills somehow, and your calls sit on their servers either way. MeetMouse never uploads anything — there's nothing to leak because nothing leaves your Mac.
  • You own it. $20 once. No plan to downgrade you, no features moving behind a paywall next quarter.

My honest verdict

Want notes? Get Fathom. Genuinely. It's free and excellent, and if my page sends you there, fine — you'll trust me on the next part.

The next part: notes don't make you better at meetings. Reading a summary of the call where you buried your ask doesn't un-bury it. If you keep seeing the same mistakes in your Fathom summaries week after week, that's the signal you need a coach, not a stenographer. MeetMouse is $20, runs quietly alongside Fathom without conflict, and if it doesn't change how your meetings go in 30 days, email me for a refund.

Questions

Is Fathom really free?
Yes — Fathom's free plan is genuinely generous for individuals, and it's the best free notetaker available. Team plans start at $19/user/month. MeetMouse isn't free ($20 one time) because it does a different job: live coaching instead of notes.
Does Fathom join your meeting as a bot?
Yes. Fathom joins as a visible recording participant and processes calls in its cloud. MeetMouse never joins the call and never uploads audio — everything runs on your Mac.
Does Fathom work offline?
No — Fathom needs a connection to record and summarize. MeetMouse transcribes and coaches entirely on-device, even with WiFi off.
What's a private alternative to Fathom?
For bot-free notes, Granola. For a fully private tool where no audio or transcript ever leaves your machine, MeetMouse — it coaches you live instead of writing notes.
