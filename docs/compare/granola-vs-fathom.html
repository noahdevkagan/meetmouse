Granola vs Fathom: no bot, or no bill?

Quick disclosure: I make MeetMouse, which competes with both of these tools sideways — it coaches you live instead of taking notes. I don't win by trashing either one, so this is the comparison I'd want if I were choosing.

TL;DR

This is bot-vs-budget. Granola captures audio on your Mac so no bot appears in the call — but the free plan keeps only 30 days of meeting history, then it's $14/user/month. Fathom is genuinely free with no caps — but a visible bot joins every meeting. Decide which cost you'd rather pay. If the answer is "neither, and I don't want my calls in any cloud," see below.

What each tool is

Granola is the Mac-native darling: it captures system audio directly on your machine — no participant named "Granola" joins — and turns your rough typed notes plus the transcript into polished meeting notes with cloud AI.

Fathom is the free workhorse: a visible bot joins the call, records it, and produces genuinely good summaries and action items at zero cost for individuals.

Where Granola wins

  • No bot, no announcement. Candidates, prospects, and nervous stakeholders never see a recorder join. This changes real conversations more than people admit.
  • The notes workflow. It enhances notes you're already typing rather than replacing you with a summary — the output feels like yours.

Where Fathom wins

  • Free means free. No history limit. Granola's free plan keeps only your last 30 days of meetings.
  • Full recordings. You get the actual video/audio to revisit or clip; Granola keeps notes, not recordings.

The part neither fixes

Bot or no bot, both generate notes with cloud AI — your meeting content leaves your machine either way. And both are documentation tools: they tell you what happened, not what to do while it's happening. MeetMouse is the third corner: like Granola there's no bot, but nothing goes to any cloud at all — transcription and AI run on your Mac, working even with WiFi off — and instead of writing notes it coaches you live for $20 once. Questions

Is Granola or Fathom better?
It's a trade: Granola takes notes with no bot joining your call but the free plan keeps only 30 days of meeting history ($14/user/month for more); Fathom is genuinely free with no caps but a visible bot joins every meeting. Pick by which cost hurts less.
Does Granola join meetings as a bot like Fathom does?
No — Granola captures system audio on your Mac, so nothing joins the call. Fathom joins as a visible recording participant.
Do Granola and Fathom work offline?
No — both generate notes with cloud AI and need a connection. The only fully offline option in this space is MeetMouse, which runs transcription and AI entirely on-device.
