MeetMouse vs Otter.ai: archive everything vs fix the meeting

Disclosure first: MeetMouse is my product. I'll tell you exactly where Otter beats it anyway, because half the people landing on this page should genuinely use Otter.

The short answer

Otter is a transcription archive — every meeting recorded, transcribed, and searchable in their cloud, for $16.99/month. MeetMouse ignores the archive entirely and coaches you live, on-device, for $20 once. If your problem is "I can't find what was said," get Otter. If your problem is "I keep saying the wrong things," get MeetMouse.

What each tool actually does

Otter is the elder statesman of meeting AI. Its bot (OtterPilot) joins your Zoom, Teams, or Meet call, records it, transcribes it in real time, and files everything into a searchable cloud workspace. Months later you can search "pricing discussion with Acme" and find the exact sentence. That archive is the product.

MeetMouse doesn't build an archive and doesn't send a bot. It runs quietly on your Mac, listens to the live conversation, and surfaces short coaching cues in an overlay only you can see — talk less, land the ask, pin down that vague commitment. Everything runs on your machine. When the meeting ends, nothing has left your Mac.

Where Otter wins

  • The searchable archive. If you need to pull receipts from a call three months ago, nothing here competes. MeetMouse deliberately keeps nothing in the cloud, so there's nothing to search later.
  • Team visibility. Shared workspaces mean your whole team can read any call. Managers love it (your reports have feelings about it, but that's between you and them).
  • Works everywhere. Phone, browser, any platform. MeetMouse is Mac-only.

Where MeetMouse wins

  • It changes the meeting you're in. Otter can transcribe you rambling with perfect accuracy. It will never tell you to stop. A cue at minute two beats a transcript at minute sixty.
  • No bot in the room. The moment "Otter.ai has joined the meeting" appears, people talk differently. MeetMouse is invisible — to your counterpart and to your screen share.
  • Privacy, for real. Every word of every Otter meeting lives on their servers. MeetMouse's transcription and AI run entirely on your Mac. WiFi off, still works. If your calls involve anything confidential, this is the difference that matters.
  • Math. Otter Pro is $204/year, every year. MeetMouse is $20, once.

My honest verdict

Otter and MeetMouse barely overlap. Otter is institutional memory; MeetMouse is personal improvement. If you're a researcher, journalist, or PM who lives in old transcripts — Otter, no hesitation, the free 300 minutes will tell you fast if you need Pro.

But if you've ever left a call knowing you talked too much, buried your ask, or accepted a "sounds good" that turned out to mean nothing — a transcript of that call is just documentation of the problem. MeetMouse is $20 to start fixing it, and if it doesn't, I'll refund you within 30 days.

Questions

Does Otter.ai join your meetings as a bot?
Yes. OtterPilot joins your Zoom, Teams, or Meet call as a visible participant and records to Otter's cloud. MeetMouse never sends a bot — it listens locally on your Mac and nothing is uploaded anywhere.
Can Otter transcribe meetings offline?
No. Otter transcribes in its cloud, so it needs an internet connection. MeetMouse runs transcription and AI entirely on your Mac — it works with WiFi off.
How much does Otter cost compared to MeetMouse?
Otter's free plan covers 300 minutes a month; Pro is $16.99/month ($204 every year). MeetMouse is $20 once, no subscription and no account.
What's the best Otter alternative that doesn't record to the cloud?
If you want meeting notes without a bot, Granola generates notes with cloud AI but never joins your call. If you want nothing to leave your machine at all, MeetMouse is the only fully on-device option — it coaches you live instead of archiving the call.
