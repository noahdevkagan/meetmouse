Fathom vs Fireflies: free and polished, or integrated and analytical?

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: Fathom is the best free notetaker — generous plan, clean summaries, easy to love as an individual. Fireflies is built for teams — deeper integrations, search, and conversation analytics, from $18/user/month. Both join as a bot and both are cloud.

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse, which competes with both. Here's the honest head-to-head first.

Both send a bot to your Zoom, Meet, or Teams call, record it, and produce summaries in their cloud. The difference is who they're optimized for: Fathom for the individual who wants great notes for free, Fireflies for the team that wants a system around its calls.
