Fathom vs Fireflies: free and polished vs built for the sales floor

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse, which competes with both. I've already said Fathom is the best free notetaker on the market and I'm not walking that back. Here's how it stacks up against Fireflies.

TL;DR

Want great notes and don't want to pay? Fathom. Stop reading. Running a sales team that wants every call scored, searchable, and in the CRM? Fireflies. Both put a bot in your call and both live in the cloud.

What each tool is

Fathom joins your Zoom, Meet, or Teams call as a bot, records it, and hands you clean summaries and action items. The free plan is absurdly generous. I don't know how their unit economics work and honestly it's not my problem.

Fireflies also joins as a bot and records, but it's built around what happens after the notes: integrations, search across every call, conversation analytics, manager dashboards.

Where Fathom wins

  • Free. You cannot out-value "unlimited and $0." Nobody can, including me.
  • Summaries. Clean and useful with zero setup.
  • For individuals. It's built for the person on the call.

Where Fireflies wins

  • Integrations. More places for your calls to go automatically.
  • Search and analytics. Better for mining calls across a whole team.
  • Built for managers. Scorecards and talk-time across reps.

The part neither fixes

Both need a bot, both keep your calls on their servers, and both tell you what happened after it's too late to change it.
