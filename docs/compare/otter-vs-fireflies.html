Otter vs Fireflies: archive for you, or machine for your team?

Quick disclosure: I make MeetMouse, which competes with both of these tools sideways — it coaches you live instead of taking notes. I don't win by trashing either one, so this is the comparison I'd want if I were choosing.

TL;DR

Otter is a personal/team transcript archive with the best search. Fireflies is sales infrastructure — CRM sync, call scoring, manager dashboards. Individuals almost always want Otter (or free Fathom); sales orgs with a pipeline want Fireflies. If being recorded into anyone's dashboard is the part you hate, see below.

What each tool is

Otter records and transcribes via its OtterPilot bot and organizes everything into a searchable workspace. It's fundamentally a memory tool — for you, and optionally your team.

Fireflies does the same recording via its bot, then goes corporate: transcripts flow into CRM records, calls get scored against keywords, managers get dashboards of talk-time and topics across every rep. It's fundamentally a management tool.

Where Otter wins

  • Simplicity and search. If the job is "find what was said," Otter does it with less machinery and less cost.
  • Individual pricing. One Pro seat is $16.99/month; Fireflies quickly becomes a per-seat org decision.

Where Fireflies wins

  • The pipeline. Automatic CRM logging, deal-stage context, coaching scorecards — if you manage revenue, this is the machine.
  • Org-wide analytics. Talk-time, keyword tracking, and trends across every rep's calls.

The part neither fixes

Both models put a bot in the room and your conversations in a company-visible cloud. Fireflies in particular means your calls are reviewable by whoever runs the dashboard — great for pipeline hygiene, less great for how it feels to be on either side of the call. Questions

Should I use Otter or Fireflies?
Individuals and small teams who mainly need transcripts and search: Otter. Sales organizations that need CRM sync, call scoring, and manager dashboards: Fireflies. If you just need free notes, Fathom beats both free plans.
Is Fireflies more expensive than Otter?
Per seat, yes at the tiers most teams need: Fireflies Pro starts at $18/user/month and Business at $59/user/month, versus Otter Pro at $16.99/month. Both bill forever; for contrast, MeetMouse is $20 once.
Do Otter and Fireflies record your meetings?
Yes — both join as a visible bot and store recordings and transcripts in their cloud; Fireflies also pushes them into your CRM. Bot-free options: Granola (cloud notes, no bot) or MeetMouse (no bot, fully on-device).
