Granola vs tl;dv: notes you read vs clips you share

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse, which competes with both sideways. Here's the straight comparison.

TL;DR

Granola if the output you want is a written note and you don't want a bot in the room. tl;dv if the output you want is "watch these 40 seconds" — it records video and makes clipping moments easy. Different outputs, different bots (as in: one has one).

What each tool is

Granola hears the call through your computer — nothing joins — and turns it plus your rough typing into polished notes. No recording kept; the note is the product.

tl;dv sends a bot that records the meeting on video, transcribes it, and makes it dead simple to clip and share moments. The recording is the product.

Where Granola wins

  • No bot. Nothing joins, nobody asks what that is.
  • Notes you'll actually read. Faster to skim than a video.
  • Cheaper paid plan. $14 vs $29.

Where tl;dv wins

  • Clips. Hearing the customer say it in their own voice beats any summary. Nobody does this better.
  • Video. Demos, screen shares, the face they made when you said the price.
  • Free recordings. Unlimited — with a 3-month shot clock. Respect, that's good business.

Questions

Is Granola or tl;dv better?
Granola for bot-free written notes. tl;dv for video recordings and shareable clips.
Does tl;dv join as a bot?
Yes, tl;dv's recorder joins the meeting. Granola captures your computer's audio instead, so nothing joins.
Does tl;dv delete free recordings?
Yes — tl;dv's free plan keeps data up to 3 months. Granola's free plan keeps 30 days of meeting history.
