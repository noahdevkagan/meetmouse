What each tool actually does

Zoom AI Companion is built into paid Zoom plans. The host turns it on and it writes meeting summaries and answers questions about the meeting — in Zoom, for Zoom meetings, in Zoom's cloud.

MeetMouse listens through your Mac, so it works in Zoom, Meet, Teams, FaceTime, Slack huddles — any app, any host. It coaches you live in an overlay hidden from screen shares, and writes notes after. Transcription and AI run on your Mac.

Frequently asked questions

Is Zoom AI Companion free?

It's included with paid Zoom plans at no extra cost. It isn't available on free Zoom accounts.

Does Zoom AI Companion work in meetings I don't host?

The host controls whether it's on. MeetMouse listens through your Mac, so it works in any meeting you can hear.

Does Zoom AI Companion coach you during the meeting?

It focuses on summaries and answering questions about the meeting. MeetMouse shows live coaching cues while you talk.

Does MeetMouse only work with Zoom?

No. It works with Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, FaceTime, Slack huddles, and anything else that plays through your Mac.
