Fathom vs tl;dv: the two best free bots

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse, which competes with both. Neither of these is a bad choice, which is kind of annoying for me.

TL;DR

Both are free, both are bots, both are good. Fathom if you want the best summaries and no expiration date on your free recordings. tl;dv if your team shares clips more than summaries. If you're still not sure, get Fathom.

What each tool is

Fathom joins your call, records it, and gives you clean summaries and action items. Generous free plan with no shot clock.

tl;dv joins your call, records it, and is built around clipping and sharing moments. The free plan keeps data up to 3 months.

Where Fathom wins

  • The free plan. No 3-month deletion. Your recordings stick around.
  • Summaries. The cleanest out of the box.
  • Cheaper paid tier. $19 vs $29.

Where tl;dv wins

  • Clipping. Grab the moment, send it to the team. That's the product.
  • Show, don't tell. For product and research teams, a 40-second clip changes minds faster than a paragraph.

Questions

Is Fathom or tl;dv better?
Fathom for summaries and a free plan that doesn't delete recordings. tl;dv for clipping and sharing moments.
Are Fathom and tl;dv free?
Both have free plans. Fathom's keeps recordings; tl;dv's free plan keeps data up to 3 months. Paid: Fathom Team from $19/user/month, tl;dv Pro $29/seat/month.
Do both join as a bot?
Yes. For no bot, look at Granola (cloud notes) or MeetMouse (on your Mac).
Can I use MeetMouse with Fathom or tl;dv?
Yes. They record; MeetMouse listens through your Mac and coaches you live. No conflict.
