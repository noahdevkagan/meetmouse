Fathom vs tl;dv: the two best free bots

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

TL;DR: Both are free, both are bots, both are good. Fathom if you want the best summaries and no expiration date on your free recordings. tl;dv if your team shares clips more than summaries. If you're still not sure, get Fathom.

Disclosure: I make MeetMouse, which competes with both. Neither of these is a bad choice, which is kind of annoying for me.

What each tool is

Fathom joins your call, records it, and gives you clean summaries and action items. Generous free plan with no shot clock.

tl;dv joins your call, records it, and is built around clipping and sharing moments. Free recordings delete after 3 months.

Where Fathom wins

  • The free plan. No 3-month deletion. Your recordings stick around.
  • Summaries. The cleanest out of the box.
  • Cheaper paid tier. $19 vs $29.

Where tl;dv wins

  • Clipping. Grab the moment, send it to the team. That's the product.
  • Show, don't tell. For product and research teams, a 40-second clip changes minds faster than a paragraph.

Both have free plans. Fathom's keeps recordings; tl;dv deletes free recordings after 3 months. Paid: Fathom Team from $19/user/month, tl;dv Pro $29/month.

Yes. For no bot, look at Granola (cloud notes) or MeetMouse (on your Mac).
