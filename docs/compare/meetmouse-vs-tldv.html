MeetMouse vs tl;dv: clips after the call vs cues during it

By Noah Kagan · Founder of MeetMouse · September 29, 2026

The short version: If your team runs on "watch these 40 seconds," get tl;dv — nobody clips meetings better. MeetMouse doesn't record video or make clips. It coaches you while the call is happening, on your Mac, for $20 once. Different jobs.

Disclosure: MeetMouse is mine. tl;dv is good at something I don't do at all, so this is an easy page to be honest on.

What each tool actually does

tl;dv sends a bot into your Zoom, Meet, or Teams call, records it, transcribes it, and makes it dead simple to clip moments and share them. Free plan with unlimited recordings that delete after 3 months; Pro is $29/month.

MeetMouse never joins the call. It listens through your Mac and shows short coaching cues in an overlay while you talk — you're monologuing, you skipped the ask, that concern never got answered. After the call you get notes with next steps and owners. Transcription and AI run on your Mac. Wi-Fi off? Still works.

Where tl;dv wins

  • Clips. Hearing the customer say it in their own voice beats any summary. For product and research teams, this is the feature.
  • Video. Demos, screen shares, the face they made when you said the price.
  • Free to start. Unlimited recordings, with a 3-month shot clock. Respect, that's good business.

Where MeetMouse wins

  • Timing. A clip of you burying your ask is still a buried ask. MeetMouse taps you while you can fix it.
  • No bot. Nothing joins, nobody asks "what's that?"
  • Privacy. No recording on anyone's server. Nothing leaves your Mac unless you choose to add your own AI key.
  • You own it. $20 once. No shot clock.
